Account & Data Deletion

How to Delete Your Account

You can request permanent deletion of your account and all associated data at any time. This can be done directly within the mobile application or by submitting an email request.

What gets permanently deleted:

  • Personal Details: Your name and email address.
  • Credentials: Your password (securely hashed credentials).
  • Profile Picture: Your uploaded custom avatar picture.
  • Location History: All logged check-ins, vibe scores, intensity levels, and time records associated with your profile.

In-App Deletion: Open the REVYND app, go to your Profile tab, select the delete account action, and confirm. All records are purged from our database immediately.

Don't have the app installed? Email us at support@revynd.com from your registered email address, and we will purge your account and records within 48 hours.
